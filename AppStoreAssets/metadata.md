# VideoNotes App Store Metadata

## Name
VideoNotes: Lecture Study

## Subtitle
Turn lecture video into notes

## Promotional Text
Scan lecture videos privately, keep the key visual moments, extract visible text on device, and assemble timestamped study cards you can revise or share.

## Description
VideoNotes turns a local lecture recording into a compact, searchable study set—without uploading the video or requiring a cloud account.

IMPORT YOUR LECTURE
Choose an MP4, MOV, M4V, or other Apple-supported video from Files or Finder. VideoNotes works directly with the file you select and keeps it under your control.

FIND THE USEFUL MOMENTS
The deterministic scanner samples the lecture timeline, retains representative visual moments, and removes exact repeats. Instead of scrubbing through an entire recording, you get a focused sequence of timestamped study cards.

ON-DEVICE OCR
Apple Vision extracts visible slide, board, diagram, and screen text locally. Each retained moment becomes a concise card with a heading, key points, and its source timestamp.

BUILT FOR STUDENTS
Turn recorded lectures, tutorials, revision sessions, conference talks, and course videos into notes you can skim before an exam. Timestamps make it easy to return to the relevant section of the original lecture.

BUILT FOR PROFESSIONALS AND EDUCATORS
Distill internal training, technical presentations, workshops, and your own educational recordings into a reusable outline. Share the complete study set as clean Markdown for editing, archiving, or use in another notes system.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Video scanning, frame selection, OCR, deduplication, and card assembly run on your Apple device. VideoNotes does not collect analytics, upload lecture files, or require an account.

SUBSCRIPTION
Start with a one-week free trial. After the trial, an active monthly VideoNotes Pro subscription is required. One subscription unlocks the complete app on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It renews automatically unless cancelled in App Store settings.
